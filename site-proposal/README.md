# Site Proposal — Belief Changer v2

This folder is the complete proposal for how the site should look, read, and feel.
One markdown file per page, in reading order. Every file shows the page top to
bottom: the actual text (as quotes), every image (as callouts), and how each
section moves. Read it like walking through the site.

**How to read the files:**

> Text in quote blocks is the actual copy that will appear on the page, verbatim.

`[IMAGE — name · size · entrance]` callouts describe the picture at that spot.
*Italic notes* describe layout, motion, and feel. Nothing here is code.

## What this proposal fixes from v1 (your complaints, mapped)

1. **"There is no text, no explanation."** The method is now the spine of the whole
   site. The homepage tells the story in six illustrated beats; how-it-works
   carries the full essay (researched: the happiest-option principle, the
   miscounted ledger, why willpower loses, why shame feeds traps, how
   understanding releases them). Real text everywhere, written to be read.
2. **"Repeat sections, bloat."** Every section now exists because it says
   something. The story beats alternate sides down the page (your zigzag),
   images are large, and no two adjacent sections share a shape.
3. **"The books are just texture files."** Every cover shown on the site gets its
   title and the series mark rendered over the artwork in the manifest's ink,
   live text, any language. Books look like books everywhere.
4. **"Light mode is broken."** Known bug (theme attribute fought over by React);
   fixed in the rebuild. Theme switching becomes the pull-cord (see 00-global).
5. **"Where are the pages we agreed on?"** They existed but were buried in the
   footer and too thin to register. Now: all pages in the main navigation, each
   with real substance, plus the new Contribute page.
6. **"Feedback should be one field."** It is now a single field with your exact
   framing, and the consent checkbox is gone.
7. **"Premium feel."** The pull-cord light switch, the hologram hover on covers,
   large images entering from the sides, a calm zigzag rhythm down the page,
   and generous scale throughout. Quiet chrome, rich moments.

## The narrative spine (why the site is ordered this way)

Everything serves one sentence: **you always choose what you believe is your
happiest option, and a trap is a belief that lies about the math.** The homepage
makes a visitor feel that in ninety seconds. How-it-works proves it in five
minutes. The books do it for real, trap by trap. The boards (requests,
experiences, feedback) let readers grow the library. About and Contribute say
who we are and invite the dedicated in. Every page answers one question and
hands you to the next.
