# Book page — one book's home

*Example used: The Scrolling Trap. The page a reader lands on from the library,
a share link, or a search engine. It must feel like holding the book in a good
bookshop: the object, the promise, and everything you can do with it.*

---

## Top (canvas, split)

*Left: the cover, LARGE (tall as the viewport allows), titled, hologram hover;
later this slot hosts the 3D book you can turn. Right, the words:*

> ## The Scrolling Trap
>
> For when the feed owns your evenings and your attention feels rented. This
> book takes the scrolling trap apart piece by piece: where the pull comes
> from, what it actually delivers, and why stopping will feel like relief
> rather than loss.
>
> *primary button:* Read online
> *secondary:* Download EPUB · Listen *(books without audio: a small yellow
> tag, "In production")*
>
> *mono line:* Version 3 · 12 languages · Improved from reader contributions

## The living book (tabs under the top, hairline)

*Two tabs: About this book · Changelog.*

**About this book**: the chapter list (readable chapters link into the reader,
the rest marked quietly), plus one paragraph:

> Like every book here, this one is alive. Readers tell us where it lost them,
> and the next version says it better. Below you can see exactly what changed
> and when, and only the newest version is ever offered for download.

**Changelog**: version rows, sparse hairlines, mono facts, no attribution ever:

> v3 · June 2026 · Rewrote chapter 4 on "boredom scrolling" · readers kept
> reporting the belief survived chapter 3
> v2 · March 2026 · Added the morning-phone chapter · the most requested gap
> v1 · January 2026 · First edition

## Help the next version (band) — THE single-field feedback

> ## Help the next version
>
> Please give us feedback. Do you have a personal experience you want to
> share? Something that did not feel described well enough for the belief to
> change? Write it here, exactly as it comes. It lands with the editors, and
> the best of it shapes the next version of this book.

*One generous textarea. One button:*

> *button:* Send it
>
> *after sending:* Thank you. Every version of this book exists because
> someone wrote in this box.

*No checkboxes, no extra fields, no consent step. Fully anonymous. (Editorial
note, not shown on page: submissions are anonymous by design; publishable
excerpts get picked editorially, always without identity.)*
