# Reader — the reading experience

*Unchanged in concept from v1 (it was the strongest surface), kept here for
completeness and with the small upgrades named.*

- Server-rendered chapters at their own URLs, indexable, per language.
- Newsreader at 65-70ch, 1.7 line height, book rhythm. Nothing else on screen:
  the chrome quiets to a hairline top bar (book title, chapter, the pull-cord
  still available) and next/previous ink links.
- **Comfort modes**: light / sepia / dark, restyling the reading surface only.
- **Continue where you left off**: position remembered per book on the device,
  and the book page's "Read online" becomes "Continue reading" when a position
  exists (small upgrade from v1).
- Chapter end: a quiet turn: next chapter link, and on the final available
  chapter of a being-written book:

> This is as far as the book goes today. It is being written in the open, and
> new chapters appear here the moment they are ready.

- At the very end of a finished book, one page of its own, calm and unadorned:

> You finished the book. If something in you has shifted, you already know.
> If you want to help the next reader, two minutes in the feedback box on the
> book's page is worth more than you think.
>
> *button:* Back to the book · *link:* Share an experience
