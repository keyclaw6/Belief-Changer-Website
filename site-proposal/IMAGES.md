## NEW paintings to generate (Voice 1, after proposal approval)

1. **The lit window** (home beat 1, "You never chose this"): a person paused on
   an evening city street, phone a soft glow in hand, warm windows above, dusk
   blues and lamp warmth, dignified, kind, not gloomy. The loop at the end of
   a day, seen without judgment.
2. **The bench** (home beat 2 + Contribute): two people on a park bench in
   morning light, one listening properly, deep greens. Being met, not judged.
3. **The open cage, painted** (home beat 4 + How-it-works ch. 6): the birdcage
   reborn in the warm painted style, door open, cage empty, the bird already
   perched on a sunlit windowsill nearby. Release, resolved.
4. **The knot, loosening** (How-it-works ch. 3): a single rope knot on a
   workbench in morning light, half loosened, one end running free toward the
   window. The mechanism, made visible.

## Cover presentation change (site-wide)

Every cover rendered anywhere gets its **title as live text over the artwork**
(upper negative space, per-book ink from the manifest) plus the small BELIEF
CHANGER series mark at the foot, in every language. Target look: the with-text
proofs in `assets/covers/proofs/`. The raw textures never appear unlabeled again.

## Prompt library (owner rule, standing)

Every generated asset's exact prompt is saved the moment it is accepted:
book covers in `skills/cover-generation/references/gallery.md` (sacred,
per-book records), site images in `skills/site-imagery/references/gallery.md`.
The four new paintings below get logged there with their full prompts and
settings on acceptance, and every future asset follows the same rule. No
accepted image without its saved prompt, ever.

## Story-telling rule (why these images and no others)

Each image exists to make one belief-change idea *felt*: the loop (lit window),
being met (bench), seeing clearly (overlook), release (cage), the mechanism
(knot), the life after (riverside). No decoration, no stock moods. If a section
cannot name the idea its image carries, it does not get an image.
