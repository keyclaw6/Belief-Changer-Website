## Exploration lineage (context for future models)

Three rounds on 2026-08-07 led here. Round 1 (10 explorations): loose watercolor
scenes, watercolor symbols, gradient veils, line+wash hybrids — owner kept only the
watercolor scenes' spirit, rejected veils and line+wash outright, and corrected the
golden-yellow haze. Round 2 (4 modern watercolors): established modern city settings,
age diversity, deeper greens/blues. Round 3 (this canon): style anchored directly on
the owner's painting; photographic voice derived from the cover grammar. The full
prompts of rejected rounds live in the project thread, deliberately not here — this
gallery records only what is canon.
