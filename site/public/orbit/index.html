Library introduction

Belief Changer is a quiet shelf of free books that help people leave traps behind — smoking, sugar, scrolling, and the rest — by seeing how the trap works.

The Orbit above is the front door: turn the ring, meet a cover, open a volume. Nothing to join. Nothing tracking you.

How it works

Each book is a short, careful argument. You read at your own pace. When a belief loosens, the behaviour that depended on it often follows without force.

Free to read

Every finished title is free forever. Drafts stay on the shelf as they are written. Scroll back up when you want another book from the ring.
